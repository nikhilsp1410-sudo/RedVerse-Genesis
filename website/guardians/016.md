## Guardian Overview
Castiel descends from the Celestial Bastion to purge corruption. Clad in the Heavenly Aegis and wielding the Seraphim's Javelin, he is a beacon of hope for the lost and a blinding terror to the wicked.

## Abilities
- **Divine Radiance:** Heals nearby allies slowly over time.
- **The Seraphim's Javelin:** Acrobatic strikes with the double-ended javelin.
- **Heaven's Judgment:** Castiel throws his javelin into the ground, unleashing a blinding wave of holy light that incinerates all enemies in a massive radius.
