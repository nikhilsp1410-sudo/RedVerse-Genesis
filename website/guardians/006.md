## Guardian Overview
Orox was not built; he was forged under the extreme pressure of a dying star during the Fracture. He is a colossal juggernaut clad in cracked obsidian plate armor laced with molten core energy. He does not dodge attacks; he absorbs them and strikes back with world-breaking force.

## Abilities
- **Tectonic Mass:** Immune to knockback, stunning, or displacement effects.
- **The Worldbreaker:** A colossal warhammer that unleashes massive shockwaves of molten energy.
- **Core Eruption:** Cracks the planet's crust and summons a massive eruption of molten gold plasma.

## Biography
When the immense gravity of the Fracture crushed a star into a sentient suit of armor, Orox was born. He has never been defeated. He simply walks through enemy lines, obliterating them with sheer mass. He is a pure force of nature.

## Lore
Orox resides in The Shattered Crucible, a dark, apocalyptic ruined city surrounded by storms and golden lightning. His weapon is not swung; it is dropped. The sheer mass of The Worldbreaker creates localized earthquakes, fracturing the tectonic plates beneath his enemies.

## Timeline
- **The Fracture:** Orox forms from a dying star.
- **The March of the Anvil:** Orox destroys the first Void fortress simply by walking through its walls.
- **The Shattering of the Crucible:** He claims a ruined, apocalyptic world as his domain.
