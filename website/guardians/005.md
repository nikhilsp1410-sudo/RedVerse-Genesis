## Guardian Overview
Forged in the upper atmospheres of the Celestial Spire, Cyrus was designed to be the ultimate vanguard. He is incredibly agile, dual-wielding plasma blades to slice through reality itself and outmaneuver the forces of the Void before they even realize they've been engaged.

## Abilities
- **Kinetic Afterimage:** Leaves explosive cyan plasma afterimages when moving at top speed.
- **The Twin Novas:** Dual energy blades that slice through spatial rifts.
- **Astral Flurry:** Unleashes a thousand perfectly aimed strikes in a single second.

## Biography
When the Architects realized they needed a rapid-response unit to counter the creeping Void, they stripped away heavy defensive plating in favor of pure kinetic potential. The result was Cyrus. He mapped the entire Celestial Spire in under a minute after his creation.

## Lore
Cyrus pushes his suit so hard that he occasionally breaks the speed of the local timeline, skipping forward a few milliseconds. This makes him nearly impossible to track and entirely unpredictable in close combat. A flash of cyan light, and the battle is over.
