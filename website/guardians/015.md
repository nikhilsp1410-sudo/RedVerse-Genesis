## Guardian Overview
Malok is the embodiment of decay. Wielding the Venomous Battleaxe, he lumbers through the Decaying Spire, turning everything he touches to ash and sludge. He is an unstoppable juggernaut of toxic energy.

## Abilities
- **Toxic Presence:** Constantly damages enemies in a wide radius.
- **The Venomous Battleaxe:** Colossal swings of his massive battleaxe.
- **The Great Plague:** Smashes his axe into the ground, causing a massive eruption of toxic sludge that severely damages and weakens all enemies.
