## Guardian Overview
When the Fracture ripped open the cosmos, the resulting entropy and death coalesced into a single entity. Moros resides in the Eclipsed Necropolis, a dark realm where the souls of shattered realities gather. He is the ultimate executioner, wielding the Eclipse Scythe to reap the dimensional energy of those who disturb the final rest of the universe.

## Abilities
- **Aura of Entropy:** Enemies near Moros slowly lose their life force. Healing is nullified.
- **The Eclipse Scythe:** Bypasses physical armor to strike directly at the target's life force.
- **Total Eclipse:** Plunges the battlefield into total darkness, instantly executing weakened enemies.

## Biography
Moros was not built; he manifested. He is the RedVerse's natural response to unchecked life and immortality. He is the personification of entropy, an unstoppable force that comes for all things eventually. He does not negotiate, and he does not show mercy.

## Lore
Moros has never spoken a word. The only sound that precedes his arrival is the heavy scraping of The Eclipse Scythe against the stone, followed by the blinding violet flash of its blade. He is the only Guardian capable of permanently severing a soul from its physical form.

## Timeline
- **The Manifestation:** Moros coalesces from the death of the first universes.
- **The Reaping of the Undying:** Moros executes a rogue faction that attempted to achieve immortality.
- **The Silent Watch:** He resides in the Necropolis, waiting for his next target.
