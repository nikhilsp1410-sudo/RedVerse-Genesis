## Guardian Overview
Tiberius stands as the ultimate defender of the Shattered Cathedral, a realm of divine order and holy retribution. Clad in the Crusader's Carapace, he wields the towering Aegis of the Lion and the devastating Sunburst Mace to repel the darkest incursions. He does not yield, and he does not break.

## Abilities
- **The Divine Bastion:** Allies behind Tiberius are shielded from harm and negative effects.
- **The Aegis of the Lion:** Plants his shield to create a massive, impenetrable wall of golden light.
- **Holy Retribution:** Releases absorbed damage in a blinding shockwave of holy amber light.

## Biography
Forged from the remnants of the Architects' holy guard, Tiberius is the unyielding shield of the innocent. During the darkest era of the Fracture, he held the Grand Nave of the Shattered Cathedral against a massive Void incursion for three centuries without taking a single step backward. 

## Lore
Tiberius is guided by The Guiding Star, a floating divine compass that points him toward those who need protection the most. He is a paragon of Lawful Good, but his rigid adherence to honor means he will face any enemy head-on, regardless of the odds.

## Timeline
- **The Holy Forging:** Tiberius is created to defend the Cathedral.
- **The Siege of Three Centuries:** He holds the gates against a massive Void army alone.
- **The Eternal Watch:** He continues to guard the most sacred realms of the RedVerse.
