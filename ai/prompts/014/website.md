# Boreas, The Glacial Sentinel
**ID:** 014
**Role:** The Vanguard

## Visual Identity
Boreas wears **The Permafrost Plate**, Dark obsidian armor covered in glowing blue geometric lines and ice-like crystals. His helmet, **The Helm of Absolute Zero**, is A pointed, bladed black crown-crest with glowing blue eyes. His chest core is **The Blue Starburst**.

## Combat
Wields **The Winter's Halberd**, A massive, jagged black and glowing blue halberd/scythe. He is accompanied by **The Frost Astrolabe**, A floating, spiked blue crystal astrolabe.
