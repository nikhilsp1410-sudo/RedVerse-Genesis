His face is completely covered by a featureless, flat mask projecting constantly shifting white and crimson binary code. The environment is a massive computational nexus, an endless digital grid of floating data-shards and geometric servers in a black void. The lighting is cold and digital, driven by the bright glow of his data-swords and the circuit-floor, casting chromatic aberration and glitchy bloom effects over his matte armor. Cyberpunk aesthetic, Blur Studio art direction, extreme hyper-detail, hologram rendering, masterpiece.
