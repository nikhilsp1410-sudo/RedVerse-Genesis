# Lore: Tiberius
## The Three-Century Siege
During the darkest era of the Fracture, a massive horde of Void entities assaulted the Shattered Cathedral. Tiberius stood alone at the gates. For three hundred years, he planted his shield in the stone and refused to move. When the dust finally settled, he was still standing there.

## The Guiding Star
The floating astrolabe that accompanies him, The Guiding Star, acts as his divine compass, pointing him toward areas of the universe where innocents are in the most desperate need of a defender.
