# Aion, The Prime Architect
**ID:** 020
**Role:** The Architect

## Visual Identity
Aion wears **The Genesis Mantle**, Blinding white and gold plate armor with intricate cosmic details. His helmet, **The Cosmic Crown**, is A sleek, hooded white and gold helmet with an enormous glowing cosmic halo. His chest core is **The Black Hole Core**.

## Combat
Wields **None**, Aion has transcended physical weapons. His hands manipulate gravity and time directly.. He is accompanied by **The Orbiting Cosmos**, Floating cosmic astrolabes, orbiting planets, and shattering geometric monoliths.
