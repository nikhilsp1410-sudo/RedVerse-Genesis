# Zael, The Crimson Scourge
**ID:** 010
**Role:** The Tormentor

## Visual Identity
Zael wears **The Flayed Carapace**, a dark, jagged suit of obsidian armor coursing with deep red glowing veins. His helmet, **The Helm of Torment**, is a jagged, bladed black crown with a glowing crimson red visor. His chest core is a glowing red spider-web star called **The Crimson Nexus**.

## Combat
Wields **The Bloodchain Whip**, a terrifying, glowing crimson red bladed chain whip that moves like a living serpent. He is accompanied by **The Eye of Ruin**, a spiked, black floating astrolabe with a crimson core.
