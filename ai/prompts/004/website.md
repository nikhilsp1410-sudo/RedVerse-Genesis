# Aurelius, The Celestial Arbiter
**ID:** 004
**Role:** The Cosmic Judge

## Visual Identity
Aurelius wears **The Empyrean Plate**, a masterpiece of brilliant white ceramic layered over a deep blue undersuit and heavily adorned with ornate gold filigree. His helmet, **The Crown of Stars**, is a pointed golden crest with bright azure glowing eyes. His chest core, **The Astral Heart**, glows brightly like a trapped star.

## Combat
Wields **The Astrolabe of Judgement**, a massive golden staff with a sunburst head containing a glowing blue core. He fights not with brute force, but with devastating celestial magic, raining azure starlight upon his foes.
