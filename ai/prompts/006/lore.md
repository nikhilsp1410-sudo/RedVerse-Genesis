# Lore: Orox
## The Sentient Star
Orox was not built by the Architects. When the Fracture occurred, the immense gravitational anomalies crushed a dying star into a dense singularity. Around this singularity, obsidian and cosmic debris formed the Tectonic Plate, creating a sentient juggernaut.

## The Unstoppable Siege
He is a pure force of nature. In battles, allied Guardians simply point him in the direction of the enemy and stay out of his way.
