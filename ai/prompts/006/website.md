# Orox, The Obsidian Anvil
**ID:** 006
**Role:** The Siege Juggernaut

## Visual Identity
Orox wears **The Tectonic Plate**, extremely heavy, layered obsidian plate armor that is fractured by glowing molten gold energy lines. His helmet, **The Crown of Cinders**, is a towering, jagged dark crown-crest with glowing molten orange eyes. His chest core is a bright, glowing molten gold sunburst called **The Furnace Heart**.

## Combat
Wields **The Worldbreaker**, a colossal, impossibly heavy obsidian warhammer containing a trapped molten core. He is accompanied by **The Aegis of Cinder**, a dark floating astrolabe-like defensive disc. He does not dodge; he absorbs all damage and obliterates his enemies with earth-shattering strikes.
