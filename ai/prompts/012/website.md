# Sylas, The Silent Judge
**ID:** 012
**Role:** The Arbiter

## Visual Identity
Sylas wears **The Cosmic Carapace**, an intricate, jagged dark armor (obsidian/black) with glowing violet outlines and gems. His helmet, **The Helm of Judgment**, is a jagged, multi-pointed gothic crown-crest with a glowing bright purple horizontal visor slit. His chest core is a bright, glowing violet starburst core.

## Combat
Wields **The Void Chakrams**, dual-wielded massive, glowing violet energy ring-blades. He is accompanied by **The Third Eye**, a third floating violet energy chakram/astrolabe above his left shoulder. He strikes with acrobatic lethality, throwing his chakrams to ricochet off surfaces.
