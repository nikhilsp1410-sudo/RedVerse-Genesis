# Lore: Kaelen
## The First Sacrifice
When the timeline fractured, the original Architects fled. Kaelen stayed behind, plunging the experimental Alpha Shard directly into his own chest armor. The resulting energy surge incinerated his physical form but bound his consciousness to the Aegis Plate forever.

## The Alpha Shard
The geometric crystal in his chest is not just a power source; it is a localized temporal anchor. As long as it burns, the Genesis Temple cannot be erased from existence.
