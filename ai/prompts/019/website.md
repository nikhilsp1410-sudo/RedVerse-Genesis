# Osiris, The Desert Mystic
**ID:** 019
**Role:** The Mystic

## Visual Identity
Osiris wears **The Sandstone Robes**, Pristine white and gold plate armor with glowing blue geometric lines and desert motifs. His helmet, **The Hood of Ra**, is A sleek, hooded white and gold helmet with a glowing blue T-shaped visor. His chest core is **The Blue Sunburst**.

## Combat
Wields **The Sun-God's Staff**, A massive golden and blue staff with a starburst ring. He is accompanied by **The Sun Dial**, A floating, intricate golden and blue astrolabe.
