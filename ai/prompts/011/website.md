# Valerius, The Blood Sentinel
**ID:** 011
**Role:** The Dark Knight

## Visual Identity
Valerius wears **The Sanguine Plate**, dark, heavy, spiked obsidian metal with a spider-web-like pattern of glowing crimson red runes. His helmet, **The Helm of the Sovereign**, is a pointed, horned demonic black helmet with glowing crimson red eyes. His chest core is a bright, glowing red star core in a web pattern known as **The Crimson Web**.

## Combat
Wields **The Bloodletter Greatsword**, a massive, broad black sword with a glowing crimson red edge and runes, capable of drawing runic circles. He is accompanied by **The Blood Diamond**, a floating, spiked dark red crystal surrounded by a spiked halo.
