# Malakor, The Void Sentinel
**ID:** 003
**Role:** The Infiltrator

## Visual Identity
Malakor wears **The Abyssal Plate**, a terrifying suit of jagged, spiked vantablack obsidian interwoven with gold filigree. His helmet, **The Crown of the Abyss**, is a towering, aggressive crest with glowing purple eyes. His chest core, **The Event Horizon**, is a literal miniature black hole swirling with deep purple plasma.

## Combat
Wields **The Singularity Spear**, an obsidian polearm with a colossal glowing purple crystal blade. He uses his companion, **The Null Sphere**, to generate protective gravity wells while he obliterates enemies with brutal melee strikes.
