A photorealistic, AAA Unreal Engine 5 cinematic 85mm profile medium close-up shot of Lyra, The Silent Judge. An elusive, ghost-like female Guardian standing on a single floating black meditation stone in a dark, infinite void. She wears semi-translucent, phase-shifted obsidian armor that looks like glass glitching out of reality. Her helmet is a completely smooth, featureless vantablack teardrop hood with faint white micro-engravings. Embedded in her chest is a flickering, glitching crimson energy core resembling digital static. She is aiming the Astral Bow, an elegant, folding mechanical bow drawing a string and arrow made of intensely bright, focused pure white and crimson light. A tiny, nearly invisible spherical drone hovers nearby, emitting faint visual sonar rings. Her body is surrounded by concentric rings of spacetime distortion, pulsing outward like ripples in water and warping the dark background. The lighting is driven almost entirely by the searing bright light of the drawn energy arrow illuminating her featureless faceplate, while a faint, ghostly cyan rim light outlines her translucent, phase-shifted back. Extremely shallow depth of field, cinematic stealth aesthetic, ethereal ghost visuals, raytraced refraction on translucent armor, masterpiece.
