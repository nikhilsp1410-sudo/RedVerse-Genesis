# Hyperion, The Golden Vanguard
**ID:** 013
**Role:** The Vanguard

## Visual Identity
Hyperion wears **The Radiant Plate**, imposing, heavy obsidian armor densely covered in jagged, glowing golden geometric lines. His helmet, **The Helm of the Sun**, is a pointed golden and black crown-crest with a glowing golden-orange T-shaped visor. His chest core is a bright gold sunburst known as **The Golden Sunburst**.

## Combat
Wields **The Solar Glaive**, a massive, ornate golden and black spear/glaive whose blade glows with golden plasma. He is accompanied by **The Sun Sphere**, a spiked, golden floating astrolabe. He fights on the frontline, using sweeping arcs of his glaive to decimate enemy lines.
