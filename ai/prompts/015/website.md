# Malok, The Toxic Juggernaut
**ID:** 015
**Role:** The Juggernaut

## Visual Identity
Malok wears **The Plague Carapace**, Dark obsidian armor with glowing toxic green spider-web lines and runes. His helmet, **The Helm of Decay**, is A horned black crown-crest with glowing toxic green eyes. His chest core is **The Toxic Core**.

## Combat
Wields **The Venomous Battleaxe**, A colossal black and green battleaxe webbed with green energy. He is accompanied by **The Plague Astrolabe**, A floating, spiked green astrolabe.
