# Kaze, The Sakura Sentinel
**ID:** 017
**Role:** The Sentinel

## Visual Identity
Kaze wears **The Lotus Plate**, Pristine, elegant white and gold plate armor with intricate details and pink gems. His helmet, **The Blossom Crest**, is An ornate white and gold helmet with a glowing pink visor and a divine glowing halo ring. His chest core is **The Sakura Jewel**.

## Combat
Wields **The Celestial War Fans**, Dual-wielded massive, elegant white, black, and gold bladed fans decorated with cherry blossoms. He is accompanied by **The Mechanical Seraph Wings**, Massive, mechanical white and gold segmented wings with glowing pink thrusters.
