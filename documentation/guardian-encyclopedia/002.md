# Valerius, The Crimson King
**ID:** 002
**Role:** Sovereign of the Timestream

## Visual Identity
Valerius wears the **God-King Plate**, a masterwork of vantablack obsidian heavily adorned with excessive, highly polished gold. His helmet, **The Crown of Hubris**, features a towering golden crest and a single horizontal crimson visor. A massive celestial clockwork halo, **The Astrolabe of Hubris**, floats behind him.

## Combat
Wields **The Sovereign Scepter**, a colossal golden halberd containing a crimson core. He fights with methodical elegance, manipulating the flow of time to strike with absolute, unavoidable precision.
