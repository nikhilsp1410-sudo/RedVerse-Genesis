# Orion, The Celestial Hunter
**ID:** 009
**Role:** The Sharpshooter

## Visual Identity
Orion wears **The Solar Carapace**, a sleek obsidian armor covered in glowing golden geometric lines. His helmet, **The Crown of Dawn**, features a winged golden crest with glowing golden-orange eyes. His chest core is a bright gold sunburst crosshair known as **The Astral Compass**.

## Combat
Wields **The Sunstrike Bow**, a massive, ornate golden and black recurve bow that fires arrows of pure golden plasma. He is accompanied by **The Eye of Apollo**, a spiked, golden floating astrolabe that acts as a targeting spotter.
