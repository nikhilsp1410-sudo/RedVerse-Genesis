# Azrael, The Cosmic Warlock
**ID:** 018
**Role:** The Warlock

## Visual Identity
Azrael wears **The Arcane Carapace**, Dark obsidian armor with glowing purple runic patterns. His helmet, **The Arcane Crest**, is A pointed, bladed black crown-crest with glowing purple eyes. His chest core is **The Purple Starburst**.

## Combat
Wields **The Nebula Scepter**, A massive black and purple staff with a glowing cosmic orb. He is accompanied by **The Cosmic Orb**, A floating, glowing purple cosmic orb.
