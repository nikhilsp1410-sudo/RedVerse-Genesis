# Castiel, The Radiant Archangel
**ID:** 016
**Role:** The Archangel

## Visual Identity
Castiel wears **The Heavenly Aegis**, Pristine white and silver plate armor with gold trim and glowing blue geometric lines. His helmet, **The Halo Crown**, is An ornate silver and white crown-crest with glowing blue eyes and a golden halo. His chest core is **The Blue Sunburst**.

## Combat
Wields **The Seraphim's Javelin**, A massive, ornate silver and blue double-ended spear. He is accompanied by **The Aegis Shield**, A floating, intricate winged silver and blue astrolabe.
