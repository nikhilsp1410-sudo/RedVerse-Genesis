# Moros, The Eclipse Wraith
**ID:** 007
**Role:** The Executioner

## Visual Identity
Moros wears **The Umbral Carapace**, a sleek, jagged suit of vantablack plate armor adorned with sharp gothic spikes and glowing violet energy veins. His helmet, **The Crown of Nightmares**, is a pointed, jagged gothic crown featuring a glowing dark purple visor. His chest core is a bright, glowing purple singularity.

## Combat
Wields **The Eclipse Scythe**, a massive, ornate dark scythe with a glowing violet plasma edge and an embedded gravity core. He is accompanied by **The Star of Ruin**, a spiked, dark, floating gothic astrolabe with a glowing violet center. He fights silently, using wide, sweeping strikes to sever the souls of his enemies.
