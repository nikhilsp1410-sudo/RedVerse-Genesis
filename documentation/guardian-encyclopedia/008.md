# Tiberius, The Golden Bulwark
**ID:** 008
**Role:** The Paladin

## Visual Identity
Tiberius wears **The Crusader's Carapace**, an imposing suit of heavy obsidian metal densely engraved with glowing gold geometric and runic patterns. His helmet, **The Helm of the Martyr**, features a pointed black and gold crown-crest with a glowing amber templar cross visor. His chest core is a bright gold sunburst known as **The Templar's Heart**.

## Combat
Wields **The Sunburst Mace** and the massive **Aegis of the Lion**, a towering dark kite shield featuring a roaring golden lion's head. He is accompanied by **The Guiding Star**, a floating golden astrolabe. He is the ultimate immovable object, planting his shield to weather any storm before striking back with holy retribution.
