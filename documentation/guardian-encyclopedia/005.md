# Cyrus, The Astral Blade
**ID:** 005
**Role:** The Vanguard

## Visual Identity
Cyrus wears **The Zenith Suit**, a highly agile, sleek armor configuration consisting of brilliant white ceramic plating over a vantablack obsidian undersuit, heavily accented with intricate gold filigree. His helmet, **The Cyan Visor**, features a pointed crown crest and a single, glowing cyan optical slit. His chest core is a bright, diamond-shaped **Cyan Star**.

## Combat
Wields **The Twin Novas**, dual-wielded ornate short swords with glowing cyan plasma edges. He is blindingly fast, relying entirely on dodging and overwhelming agility to dismantle his opponents before they can react.
