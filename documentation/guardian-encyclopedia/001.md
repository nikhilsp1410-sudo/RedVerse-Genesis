# Kaelen, The First Guardian
**ID:** 001
**Role:** The Anchor of the Timeline

## Visual Identity
Kaelen wears the **Aegis Plate**, a monolithic suit of vantablack obsidian layered with striking white ceramic plating and gold trim. Glowing crimson plasma veins pulse through the armor, terminating at the **Alpha Shard**, a massive geometric crystal core in his chest. His helmet, the **Visor of Eternity**, features a pointed crown crest and glowing red visors.

## Combat
Wields **The Chronos Halberd**, a massive polearm with a glowing crimson crystal blade. His combat style relies on impenetrable defense and single, devastating strikes that shatter time itself.
