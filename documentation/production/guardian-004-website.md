## Guardian Overview
The divine judge of the fractured timeline. Forged from pure starlight, Aurelius resides in the Astral Sanctum, maintaining absolute cosmic balance. He wields the Astrolabe of Judgement to mete out celestial punishment to those who disrupt the order of the RedVerse.

## Abilities
- **Divine Radiance:** Cleanses allies and blinds enemies with his mere presence.
- **The Astrolabe of Judgement:** Fires piercing beams of concentrated azure starlight.
- **Celestial Verdict:** Calls down a devastating orbital strike of pure light.

## Biography
When the Fracture occurred, the purest light from the tearing of the cosmos coalesced into Aurelius. He constructed the Astral Sanctum from the debris of dead universes, creating a flawless golden realm where he observes the timeline.

## Lore
Aurelius does not care about good or evil; he cares only about balance. He has destroyed entire civilizations that tipped the scales too far toward chaos, and he will not hesitate to strike down his fellow Guardians if they threaten the timeline. The Prism of Truth reveals the sins of all who stand before him.

## Timeline

- **The Foundation:** He constructs the Astral Sanctum.
- **The First Judgement:** He banishes the early chaotic forces from the golden realm.
