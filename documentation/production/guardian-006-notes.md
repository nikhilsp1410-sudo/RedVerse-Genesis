# Production Notes: Guardian 006

## Art Direction
Based strictly on the approved final asset:
- Extremely heavy, massive, imposing silhouette.
- Dark obsidian and vantablack contrasted strongly with glowing molten gold/orange.
- Jagged, towering crown helmet with glowing eyes.
- The weapon is a massive, blocky obsidian hammer with a molten core.
- The environment is apocalyptic, ruined, and stormy with golden lightning.
- Visually unique from 001-005: 004 is divine white/gold, 005 is sleek white/cyan. 006 is massive obsidian/molten gold.
