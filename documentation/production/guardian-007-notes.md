# Production Notes: Guardian 007

## Art Direction
Based strictly on the approved final asset:
- Dark, gothic, grim reaper aesthetic.
- The armor is jagged vantablack with sharp spikes.
- The weapon is a massive, ornate scythe with a glowing violet blade.
- The cape must be heavily tattered and dissolving into purple mist/lightning at the edges.
- The environment is a purple, stormy gothic necropolis with a dark eclipsed moon and floating spires.
- Visually unique from 001-006: He is the first heavily gothic, reaper-themed Guardian, utilizing dark purple energy and a scythe.
