## Guardian Overview
An imposing force of chaotic justice, Malakor willingly embraced the darkness of the Void to protect reality from its horrors. Bound to a collapsed star, he wields unimaginable gravitational power.

## Abilities
- **Gravitational Pull:** Enemies are relentlessly dragged into his range.
- **The Singularity Spear:** Devastating melee strikes that crush targets under their own mass.
- **Event Horizon Collapse:** Annihilates the surrounding area with a localized black hole.

## Biography
During the Fracture, Malakor realized that defensive tactics would inevitably fail. He made the ultimate sacrifice, plunging himself directly into the Void. To survive, he consumed a collapsed star, binding its gravity to his armor.

## Lore
The black hole in his chest grants him unimaginable power, but it is a curse. It constantly pulls at his sanity and his soul. He fights a two-front war: one against the Void Walkers, and one against himself.

## Timeline
- **The Fracture:** Malakor abandons his post.
- **The Descent:** He plunges into the Void.
- **The Binding:** He consumes a collapsed star to survive, becoming the Void Sentinel.
