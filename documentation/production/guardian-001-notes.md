# Production Notes: Guardian 001

## Art Direction
Based strictly on the approved final asset:
- The white ceramic plating over the vantablack obsidian is a key defining feature and must contrast heavily.
- The red plasma veins must glow intensely, looking like liquid energy flowing through the armor.
- The Alpha Shard is a prominent, large geometric crystal in the center of the chest.
- Ordis (the floating sphere) should have intersecting rings.
- The environment is dark, ancient ruins under a celestial beam of light.
