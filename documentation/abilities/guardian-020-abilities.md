# Abilities: Aion

## Passive: Omnipresence
Aion is everywhere and nowhere. He cannot be surprised.

## Primary Weapon: None
Alters reality to erase enemies from existence with a wave of his hand.

## Secondary: The Orbiting Cosmos
Summons collapsing stars.

## Ultimate: The Big Bang
Aion completely resets the battlefield, restoring his allies to full strength and utterly annihilating all enemies at a sub-atomic level.
