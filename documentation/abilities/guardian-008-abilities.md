# Abilities: Tiberius

## Passive: The Divine Bastion
Allies standing behind Tiberius gain a massive boost to their defensive stats and are shielded from negative status effects.

## Primary Weapon: The Sunburst Mace
A heavy, spiked mace that deals bonus holy damage to corrupted or chaotic enemies.

## Secondary: The Aegis of the Lion
Tiberius plants his shield, creating a massive, impenetrable wall of golden light that absorbs all incoming frontal damage.

## Ultimate: Holy Retribution
Tiberius absorbs damage into his shield and releases it all in a devastating, blinding shockwave of amber light that obliterates enemies and heals allies.
