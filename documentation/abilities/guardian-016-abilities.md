# Abilities: Castiel

## Passive: Divine Radiance
Heals nearby allies slowly over time.

## Primary Weapon: The Seraphim's Javelin
Acrobatic strikes with the double-ended javelin.

## Secondary: The Aegis Shield
His floating shield blocks all incoming projectiles.

## Ultimate: Heaven's Judgment
Castiel throws his javelin into the ground, unleashing a blinding wave of holy light that incinerates all enemies in a massive radius.
