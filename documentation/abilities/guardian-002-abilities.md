# Abilities: Valerius

## Passive: Aura of Majesty
Enemies within his presence find their movements slowed by time dilation and the sheer weight of his imposing aura.

## Primary Weapon: The Sovereign Scepter
A massive polearm that does not cut flesh, but severs the target's connection to their immediate future, causing delayed, devastating wounds.

## Ultimate: The Golden Hour
Valerius reverses time in a localized area, restoring his own armor while freezing his enemies in golden stasis.
