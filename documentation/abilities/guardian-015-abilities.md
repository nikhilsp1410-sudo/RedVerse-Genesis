# Abilities: Malok

## Passive: Toxic Presence
Constantly damages enemies in a wide radius.

## Primary Weapon: The Venomous Battleaxe
Colossal swings of his massive battleaxe.

## Secondary: The Plague Astrolabe
Spreads toxic spores that blind enemies.

## Ultimate: The Great Plague
Smashes his axe into the ground, causing a massive eruption of toxic sludge that severely damages and weakens all enemies.
