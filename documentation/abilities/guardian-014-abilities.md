# Abilities: Boreas

## Passive: Absolute Zero
Enemies near Boreas are slowed significantly.

## Primary Weapon: The Winter's Halberd
Sweeping strikes with the massive halberd that shatter frozen enemies.

## Secondary: The Frost Astrolabe
Fires piercing icicles.

## Ultimate: Glacial Epoch
Boreas freezes the entire battlefield, completely stopping all enemy movement for a short duration.
