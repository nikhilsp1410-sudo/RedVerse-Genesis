# Abilities: Cyrus

## Passive: Kinetic Afterimage
When moving at top speed, Cyrus leaves behind cyan plasma afterimages that confuse enemies and explode upon contact.

## Primary Weapon: The Twin Novas
Dual energy blades that slice through physical matter and spatial rifts with zero resistance.

## Ultimate: Astral Flurry
Cyrus moves so fast he exists in multiple places at once, unleashing a thousand perfectly aimed strikes across a wide area in a single second.
