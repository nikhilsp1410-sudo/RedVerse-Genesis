# Abilities: Sylas

## Passive: Cosmic Momentum
Sylas gains speed and evasion every time he successfully catches one of his returning chakrams.

## Primary Weapon: The Void Chakrams
Dual energy ring-blades that can be used for rapid melee slashes or thrown as deadly boomerangs.

## Secondary: The Third Eye
His floating astrolabe can autonomously intercept incoming projectiles.

## Ultimate: Tribunal's Verdict
Sylas throws both chakrams into the air, where they multiply into dozens of ricocheting energy rings that bounce violently around the battlefield, slicing through all enemies.
