# Abilities: Zael

## Passive: Bloodlust
Zael's speed and attack power increase as he and his enemies take damage.

## Primary Weapon: The Bloodchain Whip
A terrifying chain-scythe that extends to incredible lengths, slicing through armor and inflicting deep, bleeding wounds.

## Secondary: The Eye of Ruin
Fires piercing crimson lasers that lock targets in place for his whip.

## Ultimate: The Red Slaughter
Zael spins the Bloodchain Whip at impossible speeds, creating a massive tornado of crimson blades that shreds everything in a wide radius.
