# Abilities: Hyperion

## Passive: Vanguard's Presence
Allies near Hyperion gain increased attack speed and movement speed.

## Primary Weapon: The Solar Glaive
A massive polearm that unleashes arcs of golden plasma with every sweeping strike.

## Secondary: The Sun Sphere
His floating astrolabe acts as a beacon, periodically emitting blinding flashes of light to disorient enemies.

## Ultimate: Solar Charge
Hyperion lowers his glaive and charges forward with unstoppable momentum, leaving a trail of burning golden plasma in his wake and knocking all enemies into the air.
