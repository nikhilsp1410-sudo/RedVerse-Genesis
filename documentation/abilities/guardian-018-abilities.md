# Abilities: Azrael

## Passive: Arcane Mastery
Spells have a chance to instantly kill lesser enemies.

## Primary Weapon: The Nebula Scepter
Fires bolts of concentrated dark energy from his scepter.

## Secondary: The Cosmic Orb
Summons minor black holes to trap enemies.

## Ultimate: Supernova
Unleashes the full power of a dying star, completely obliterating everything on the battlefield in a massive wave of purple cosmic fire.
