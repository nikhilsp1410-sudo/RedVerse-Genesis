# Abilities: Orox

## Passive: Tectonic Mass
Orox is completely immune to knockback, stunning, or displacement effects. His heavy footsteps create small shockwaves.

## Primary Weapon: The Worldbreaker
A colossal warhammer that unleashes a massive shockwave of molten energy upon impact.

## Secondary: The Aegis of Cinder
A floating defensive disc that intercepts incoming projectiles and melts them into slag.

## Ultimate: Core Eruption
Orox slams The Worldbreaker into the ground, cracking the planet's crust and summoning a massive eruption of molten gold plasma that incinerates everything in a huge radius.
