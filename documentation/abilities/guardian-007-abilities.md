# Abilities: Moros

## Passive: Aura of Entropy
Enemies standing near Moros slowly lose their life force and dimensional energy. Healing effects are nullified in his presence.

## Primary Weapon: The Eclipse Scythe
A massive scythe that bypasses physical armor entirely, striking directly at the target's life force.

## Secondary: The Star of Ruin
A floating gothic astrolabe that can fire concentrated beams of dark matter, pulling enemies toward Moros.

## Ultimate: Total Eclipse
Moros summons a massive dark eclipse that plunges the battlefield into total darkness. In the darkness, he strikes every enemy simultaneously, instantly executing those whose energy is low.
