# Abilities: Kaze

## Passive: Tailwind
Kaze's movement speed increases with every successful strike.

## Primary Weapon: The Celestial War Fans
Flurries of razor-sharp wind blades from his dual fans.

## Secondary: The Mechanical Seraph Wings
Uses his mechanical wings to dash incredible distances.

## Ultimate: The Thousand Petal Storm
Kaze spins at blinding speeds, creating a massive tornado of razor-sharp cherry blossoms that shreds everything in its path.
