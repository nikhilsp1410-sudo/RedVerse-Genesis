# Abilities: Orion

## Passive: Eagle's Eye
Orion's vision allows him to see perfectly through illusions, dimensional tears, and solid objects up to a cosmic distance.

## Primary Weapon: The Sunstrike Bow
Fires bolts of hyper-concentrated golden plasma that travel at the speed of light.

## Secondary: The Eye of Apollo
His floating astrolabe spotter can project tracking tethers onto targets, ensuring his arrows never miss.

## Ultimate: Celestial Rain
Orion fires a single arrow into the sky that shatters into a million golden plasma bolts, raining down destruction upon a massive area.
