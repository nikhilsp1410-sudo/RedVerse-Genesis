# Abilities: Osiris

## Passive: Solar Flare
Enemies that attack Osiris are temporarily blinded.

## Primary Weapon: The Sun-God's Staff
Fires concentrated beams of solar energy from his staff.

## Secondary: The Sun Dial
Summons shifting sands to trap enemies.

## Ultimate: Wrath of the Sun God
Osiris calls down a massive pillar of searing solar light from the heavens, incinerating all enemies caught in its radius.
