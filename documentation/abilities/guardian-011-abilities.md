# Abilities: Valerius

## Passive: Blood Siphon
Enemies within Valerius's runic circles slowly lose health, which heals Valerius.

## Primary Weapon: The Bloodletter Greatsword
A massive heavy blade that can slice through multiple armored targets in a single swing.

## Secondary: The Blood Diamond
Fires concentrated blasts of dark red energy at targets caught in his runes.

## Ultimate: Runic Subjugation
Valerius plunges his greatsword into the ground, activating a massive summoning circle that paralyzes all enemies and drains their dimensional energy.
