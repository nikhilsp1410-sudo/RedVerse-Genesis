# Abilities: Aurelius

## Passive: Divine Radiance
Allies near Aurelius are cleansed of corruption and negative status effects, while enemies are blinded by his glowing presence.

## Primary Weapon: The Astrolabe of Judgement
Fires piercing beams of concentrated azure starlight that ignore physical armor and burn the target's soul.

## Ultimate: Celestial Verdict
Aurelius summons a massive golden astrolabe in the sky above the battlefield, which calculates the sins of his enemies and obliterates them with a devastating orbital strike of pure light.
