# Abilities: Kaelen

## Passive: Temporal Stasis
Kaelen cannot be moved, staggered, or displaced by physical or magical force while his feet are planted.

## Primary Weapon: The Chronos Halberd
A devastating plasma-infused strike that leaves a lingering rift in the air, damaging enemies who pass through the trajectory of the swing.

## Ultimate: Time Dilation Strike
Kaelen becomes completely immobile, drawing all ambient temporal energy into the Alpha Shard before releasing an omnidirectional shockwave of pure chronological force, freezing enemies in time.
