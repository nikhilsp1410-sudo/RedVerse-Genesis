# Abilities: Malakor

## Passive: Gravitational Pull
Enemies in close proximity are slowly dragged toward Malakor, making escape impossible.

## Primary Weapon: The Singularity Spear
Each strike from the spear increases the target's mass, crushing them under their own weight.

## Ultimate: Event Horizon Collapse
Malakor releases the singularity in his chest, creating a massive, localized black hole that completely annihilates everything within its radius before collapsing back into his armor.
