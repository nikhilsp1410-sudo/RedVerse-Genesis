# Guardian 006: Orox (The Obsidian Anvil)

## The Shattered Crucible
Orox resides in The Shattered Crucible, a dark, apocalyptic ruined city surrounded by storms and golden lightning. It is a harsh environment where the gravity is crushing and the heat is unbearable for anyone else.

## The Worldbreaker
His weapon is not swung; it is dropped. The sheer mass of The Worldbreaker creates localized earthquakes, fracturing the tectonic plates beneath his enemies.
