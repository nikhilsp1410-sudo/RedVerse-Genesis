# Guardian 008: Tiberius (The Golden Bulwark)

## The Shattered Cathedral
Tiberius serves as the eternal guard of the Shattered Cathedral, a dark, imposing gothic realm of towering black spires and ancient knight statues, illuminated by storms of amber divine light.

## The Aegis of the Lion
His shield is not just metal; it is a holy relic capable of projecting massive barriers of golden light that can repel even the most devastating cosmic attacks.
