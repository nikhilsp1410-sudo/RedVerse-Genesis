# Visual Identity Bible

## 1. Core Aesthetic
**AAA Unreal Engine 5 Realism & Dark Luxury.** The RedVerse feels like a museum-quality cinematic collectible. Ancient Futurism perfectly blended with sacred technology.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary**: Vantablack, Obsidian Dark Grey, pure Black.
- **Secondary**: Polished Titanium, Gold, Silver, Platinum (used sparingly for luxury accents).
- **Accent/Energy**: Crimson Red (#DC143C) to Deep Blood Red.

## 3. Shape Language
- Brutalist but incredibly refined and luxurious.
- Monolithic and immense scale.
- Floating components held together by Crimson Energy fields.
- Microscopic, hyper-detailed surface materials.

## 4. Unacceptable Elements
- NO anime or cartoon styling.
- NO low-detail concepts or rough sketches.
- NO generic cyberpunk (no neon signs, dirty streets, or punk aesthetics).
- NO rust, dirt, or decay. This is sacred, eternal technology.
