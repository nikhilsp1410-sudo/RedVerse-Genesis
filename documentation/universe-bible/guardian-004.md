# Guardian 004: Aurelius (The Celestial Arbiter)

## The Astral Sanctum
Aurelius observes the multiverse from the Astral Sanctum, a heavenly ancient realm of gold architecture, floating blue crystals, and massive celestial rings in the sky. It is a place of absolute perfection and order.

## The Prism of Truth
He is accompanied by the **Prism of Truth**, a glowing blue diamond that floats above his hand. It reveals the true alignment and past sins of anyone he looks upon, guiding his absolute judgment.
