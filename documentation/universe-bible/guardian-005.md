# Guardian 005: Cyrus (The Astral Blade)

## The Celestial Spire
Cyrus operates in the upper atmosphere of the Celestial Spire, a bright heavenly city of towering pillars and floating geometry. He is the first line of defense, patrolling the skies with unmatched velocity.

## The Celestial Gyro
He is accompanied by **The Celestial Gyro**, a floating astrolabe-like geometric sphere with a glowing cyan core and intersecting rings that helps him calculate complex spatial trajectories at hyper-speeds.
