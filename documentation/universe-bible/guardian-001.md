# Guardian 001: Kaelen (The First Guardian)

## The Genesis Temple
Kaelen stands eternal guard at the Genesis Temple, the ancient monolithic ruin at the epicenter of the Fracture. Under a starry cosmic sky, surrounded by floating shards of reality, he holds the timeline together through sheer willpower.

## Companion
He is accompanied by **Sentinel Drone Ordis**, a floating dark geometric sphere with a glowing crimson core and intersecting orbital rings, which acts as an extension of his sensory perception.
