# Technology & Magic System

## Ancient Futurism
In the RedVerse, magic and technology are indistinguishable. The architecture is built from Black Obsidian, but it floats and shifts using Crimson Energy.

## Sacred Tech
Weapons and armor are not just manufactured; they are forged from the remnants of dead stars and powered by fragments of reality itself.

## The Crimson Energy
This is not electricity or plasma. It is the raw fabric of existence. It can bend time, alter gravity, and rewrite physical matter. It appears visually as a volumetric, glowing crimson light, often behaving like a fluid or slow-moving fire.
