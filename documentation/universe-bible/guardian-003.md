# Guardian 003: Malakor (The Void Sentinel)

## The Void Rifts
Malakor operates entirely outside the normal reality, roaming the surreal, shattered landscapes of the Void Rifts. Surrounded by floating black monoliths and massive swirling purple cosmic nebulas, he hunts the Void Walkers on their own turf.

## The Null Sphere
He is accompanied by the **Null Sphere**, a dark floating orb that acts as a gravitational anchor, preventing the chaotic forces of the Void from tearing him apart.
