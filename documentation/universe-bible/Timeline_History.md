# Timeline & History

## 1. Era of the Architects
A time of absolute technological supremacy. The Architects mapped the universe and discovered the living cosmic force: The Crimson Core.

## 2. The Crimson Fracture (Year Zero)
The day reality shattered. The Architects' attempt to control the Core caused it to explode. The shockwave collapsed dimensions and erased civilizations from time. 

## 3. The Silence
A dark age spanning millennia where the twenty surviving Guardians wandered the fractured dimensions, gathering their strength and learning to wield their Crimson Shards.

## 4. The Awakening (Present Day)
The Guardians have begun to reunite. The fractured reality is bleeding into new dimensions. The search for the instigator of the Fracture begins.
