# Guardian 007: Moros (The Eclipse Wraith)

## The Eclipsed Necropolis
Moros resides in The Eclipsed Necropolis, a dark, gothic nightmare realm characterized by floating black spires, massive iron chains scattered across the ground, and a perpetual dark eclipsed moon dominating the stormy purple sky. It is the final resting place of shattered realities.

## The Inevitable
Moros does not negotiate, and he does not show mercy. He is the personification of entropy. He is the only Guardian capable of permanently severing a soul from its physical form.
