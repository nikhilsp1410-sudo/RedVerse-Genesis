# RedVerse Canon - Single Source of Truth

## The Premise
Long before humanity existed, an ancient civilization called The Architects discovered a living cosmic force known as the Crimson Core. The Crimson Core is not merely an energy source; it is the living heart of reality itself.

## The Crimson Fracture
The Architects attempted to harness and control the Crimson Core to achieve supreme dimensional dominance. The experiment failed catastrophically. The Core shattered in an event known as The Crimson Fracture. Reality itself fractured. Entire civilizations vanished. Dimensions collapsed. Time became unstable.

## The Guardian Order
Only twenty legendary Guardians survived the cataclysm. Each Guardian possesses one Crimson Shard—a fragment of the original Crimson Core. Individually, they preserve reality. Together, they possess enough power to either restore existence or destroy it forever.

## The Mystery
The true identity of the one responsible for the Crimson Fracture remains the greatest mystery in the RedVerse. Uncovering this secret is the central arc of the Genesis collection and future expansions.
