# The Guardian Order

The Guardian Order consists of exactly twenty unique, legendary beings. There are no duplicates and no procedurally generated grunts in this collection.

## Crimson Shards
Each Guardian is powered by a Crimson Shard, a fragment of the shattered living heart of reality. This shard provides them with their signature abilities, their auras, and their immortality.

## Factions / Alignments
While they are all Guardians, they do not all share the same moral alignment. Some seek to restore reality; others seek to rule the fractured dimensions; some are agents of pure chaos.
