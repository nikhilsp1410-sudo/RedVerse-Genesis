# Guardian 002: Valerius (The Crimson King)

## The Hall of Eternity
Valerius rules from a fractured pocket dimension known as the Hall of Eternity. It is an endless, opulent golden hall filled with towering hourglasses that measure the lifespan of dying universes. Valerius stands at its center, using the Astrolabe of Hubris to manipulate reality.

## The Rivalry
Valerius holds deep disdain for Kaelen (Guardian 001). While Kaelen sacrificed himself to stabilize the timeline, Valerius seeks to exploit the fractured timelines for personal worship and glory.
