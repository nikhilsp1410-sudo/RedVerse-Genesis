# Lore: Moros
## The Manifestation of Entropy
When the Fracture ripped open the cosmos, the resulting death and destruction coalesced into a single entity. Moros was not built; he manifested. He is the RedVerse's natural response to unchecked life and immortality.

## The Silent Reaper
Moros has never spoken a word. The only sound that precedes his arrival is the heavy scraping of The Eclipse Scythe against the stone, followed by the blinding violet flash of its blade.
