# Lore: Cyrus
## Forged for Speed
When the Architects realized they needed a rapid-response unit to counter the creeping Void, they stripped away heavy defensive plating in favor of pure kinetic potential. The result was Cyrus.

## The Time Skips
Cyrus pushes the Zenith Suit so hard that he occasionally breaks the speed of the local timeline, skipping forward a few milliseconds. This makes him nearly impossible to track and entirely unpredictable in close combat.
