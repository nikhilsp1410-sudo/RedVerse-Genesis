# Lore: Aurelius
## The Manifestation
Unlike the other Guardians who were forged or corrupted, Aurelius manifested directly from the pure light released during the Fracture. He is the universe's immune system, created to restore order to the chaos.

## The Absolute Law
Aurelius does not care about good or evil; he cares only about balance. He has destroyed entire civilizations that tipped the scales too far toward chaos, and he will not hesitate to strike down his fellow Guardians if they threaten the timeline.
