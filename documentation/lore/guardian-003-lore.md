# Lore: Malakor
## The Descent
When the Fracture tore reality apart, Malakor realized that defensive tactics would inevitably fail. He made the ultimate sacrifice, plunging himself directly into the Void. To survive, he consumed a collapsed star, binding its gravity to his armor.

## The Event Horizon
The black hole in his chest grants him unimaginable power, but it is a curse. It constantly pulls at his sanity and his soul. He fights a two-front war: one against the Void Walkers, and one against himself.
