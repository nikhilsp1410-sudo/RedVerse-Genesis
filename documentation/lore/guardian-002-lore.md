# Lore: Valerius
## The Forging of the Crown
When the Fracture occurred, Valerius did not try to stop it. Instead, he reached into the cosmic tear and forged the Crown of Hubris from the raw energy of dying stars. 

## The Astrolabe of Hubris
The massive clockwork halo behind him is not just decorative; it is a functioning celestial engine. It calculates the precise degradation of reality, allowing Valerius to step between seconds.
